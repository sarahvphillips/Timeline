## Full description

```
Timeline is a private life log. It keeps your notes, people, poems and dates in one place, sorted by year, month and week.

What you can keep
• Events and notes, with a category and a label
• Poems, with a type you choose
• People, including friends who do not use Timeline yet
• Days between dates, a date circle, and word-to-number lists
• Optional household notes, such as wash loads and food
• A single event shared with someone you invite

Nothing is public unless you turn that on for your own profile.

Your account
Sign in with email. Photos and recordings stay on your device in this version. You can delete the account in Settings. That removes the login and the timeline data stored for it.

We do not show ads. We do not sell your data. We do not use your events to train public AI models.

Timeline is not for children under 13.

Made by Sarah Phillips in the United Kingdom.
Privacy policy: https://github.com/sarahvphillips/Timeline/blob/main/docs/privacy.html
Contact: sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com
```
