# Delete your TimelineApp account

**App:** TimelineApp (package `com.sarahphillips.timelineapp`)  
**Developer:** Sarah Phillips  
**Updated:** 26 September 2026

This page is for people who used TimelineApp on Google Play and want their account and the data stored with it removed.

## How to request deletion

Use either method. Both apply to the same TimelineApp account.

### In the app (fastest)

1. Open **TimelineApp**.
2. Sign in with the email you want removed.
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete account**.
5. Read the warning, type your password if asked, and confirm.

Deletion starts at once. You are signed out when it finishes.

### By email

1. Write to [sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com](mailto:sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20TimelineApp%20account).
2. Use the subject line: **Delete my TimelineApp account**.
3. Send it from the same email as the account, or name that email in the message.

Email requests are handled within 30 days.

## Data that is deleted

- Your Firebase login (email and password, or Google sign-in for this app).
- Your Timeline events, poems, notes, Word-to-Int list, settings, and signed-in device list stored in the cloud.
- Shares and invites you created.
- A public profile or username if you had one.
- Credits and stamp records held for that account.
- The Timeline copy stored on the device you used to delete the account.

## Data that is not deleted, or is kept for a short time

- Photos, videos, and audio that stayed in your phone or laptop gallery. Timeline does not overwrite those files. Remove them in the gallery if you want them gone.
- Events another person saved on their own Timeline after you shared them. Their copy is part of their account. They can delete it themselves.
- Guest mode data, which never had an account. Uninstall the app or use Leave guest mode on that device.
- A short record of an email deletion request (from, date, and that it was done) for up to 30 days, then it is removed unless the law says it must be kept.
- Google Play purchase records stay with Google, not with TimelineApp. In-app credit packs are off during testing.
- A cache on another phone or browser may remain until that copy of the app is opened after the cloud account is gone.

## How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. An email request is completed within 30 days. After that we do not keep your timeline.
