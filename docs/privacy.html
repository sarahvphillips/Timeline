TimelineApp privacy policy

Version 1.1 · Updated 24 September 2026

TimelineApp (“Timeline”) is a personal life-log. It is made by Sarah Phillips in the United Kingdom. Contact: sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, Google sign-in if you choose it, and a Firebase user id.
  • What you type: events, poems, SMS and call notes you enter, people, labels, Word-to-Int, date circle, food, wash loads, purchases, banking notes, and media links.
  • During testing, your view on whether a later credit price feels fair. That note is stored with your account.
  • App data: credits, stamps, perks, invites, shares, device sessions, and settings.

We do not ask for bank passwords, card numbers, or Google Play payment details.

Photos, video, and audio

Pictures, wash photos, and recordings are meant to stay on your device. They are not uploaded to Firebase Storage in this version. A SHA-256 checksum of a clip you choose may be stored. Original gallery files are not overwritten.

Gmail and Open Banking

Gmail is used only if you choose it. Open Banking is a sandbox until a regulated provider is connected. A live bank login would be on the bank’s own page, never inside Timeline.

Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) plus a copy on the device. Google Ireland / Google LLC processes that data. Credit packs are turned off while the app is in testing. When they return, they will use Google Play Billing on Android only.

Sharing

Nothing is public by default. You share specific events with Timeline friends you invite. Credits can be transferred to another Timeline email.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. In the app, Settings → Delete account removes the Firebase login, that account’s timeline data in Firestore, shares and invites you created, a public profile if you had one, and the copy on that device. Clearing the local cache does not delete the account.

You can also email the contact above, or complain to the ICO.

UK GDPR

  • Contract: the account you asked for.
  • Consent: camera, photos, Gmail, and a future bank connection.
  • Legitimate interests: security (sessions, admin audit, checksums).

Children

Timeline is not for children under 13.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, run ads, or use your events to train public AI models.
