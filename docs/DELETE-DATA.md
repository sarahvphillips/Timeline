# Request deletion of your TimelineApp data

**App:** TimelineApp (package `com.sarahphillips.timelineapp`)  
**Developer:** Sarah Phillips  
**Updated:** 26 September 2026

This page is for people who used TimelineApp on Google Play and want some or all of their data removed. You can do this without closing the account. Closing the account is a separate step at the end.

## How to request deletion

### In the app — delete some data, keep the account

1. Open **TimelineApp** and sign in.
2. Open **Timeline**.
3. Open the event, poem, or other item you want gone.
4. Tap **Delete** (or **Leave event** if it was shared with you) and confirm.

Other in-app steps that do not close the account:

- **Profile photo:** tap the photo on Home and choose remove.
- **This device’s copy only:** Settings → clear local cache. Cloud data for the account stays until you delete those items or the account.
- **Signed-in devices:** Home or Settings → remove a device from the list.

### By email — ask us to delete named data

1. Write to [sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com](mailto:sarah.v.phillips@googlemail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20TimelineApp%20data).
2. Use the subject line: **Delete my TimelineApp data**.
3. Send it from the same email as the account, or name that email.
4. Say what you want removed (for example: all events, one date, Word-to-Int list, device sessions). Say if you want to **keep the account**.

Email requests are handled within 30 days.

### Delete everything, including the account

1. Settings → **Delete account**, type DELETE, and confirm.  
   Or follow [Delete your TimelineApp account](DELETE-ACCOUNT.md).

## Data that can be deleted while the account stays open

- Individual events, poems, notes, and other timeline items you delete in the app.
- A shared event you leave (your copy only).
- Profile photo stored by TimelineApp.
- Word-to-Int rows you delete in that screen.
- Device-session rows you remove.
- The local cache on the device you clear in Settings.

## Data that is deleted only when the account is deleted

- The Firebase login.
- The remaining cloud timeline, settings, shares and invites you created, public profile, and credits for that account.

## Data that is not deleted, or is kept for a short time

- Photos, videos, and audio that stayed in your phone or laptop gallery. Remove those in the gallery if you want them gone.
- Copies another person saved on their own Timeline after you shared an item.
- Guest mode data on a device (no account). Uninstall the app or use Leave guest mode.
- A short record of an email request (from, date, and that it was done) for up to 30 days, then it is removed unless the law says it must be kept.
- Google Play purchase records stay with Google. Credit packs are off during testing.

## How long it takes

In-app deletes are immediate. An email request is completed within 30 days.
